## Key Features Demonstrated

### 1. **Time Progression**
- Start in 8000 BC
- Progress year by year through the Neolithic era
- Track progress toward Iron Age (1200 BC)

### 2. **Resource Management**
- Food: Essential for survival
- Wood: Used for construction
- Stone: Required for advanced buildings
- Clay: Needed for pottery and writing
- Hides: From hunting, used for technologies

### 3. **Technology Tree**
- Start with basic technologies (fire, stone tools, hunting, gathering)
- Research progressively advanced technologies
- Technologies unlock new capabilities (farming, writing, metalworking)
- Organized by historical eras (Early/Middle/Late Neolithic, Chalcolithic)

### 4. **Agriculture and Food Production**
- Build farms to increase food production
- Farming level improves with experience
- Balance between gathering and agriculture

### 5. **Population Dynamics**
- Natural population growth
- Deaths from starvation if food is insufficient
- Population requirements for advanced governance

### 6. **Geography and Migration**
- Multiple location types with different characteristics
- Migration system with risk/reward mechanics
- Climate effects on resources

### 7. **Governance Evolution**
- Tribal Elder → Chieftain → Council → Priest-King → Early State
- Population and technology requirements
- Stability and production modifiers

### 8. **Writing System**
- Proto-writing leads to true writing
- Writing unlocks advanced governance
- Cultural development tracking

### 9. **Seasonal Effects**
- Four seasons with different production rates
- Winter reduces food production
- Spring/summer increase yields

### 10. **Random Events**
- Good harvests
- Harsh winters
- Wildlife discoveries
- Disease outbreaks

## Victory Conditions

The game allows free-form play through the Neolithic period. Success can be measured by:
- Reaching the Iron Age with a thriving civilization
- Maximizing population growth
- Discovering all available technologies
- Accumulating culture points
- Achieving advanced governance systems
